What you get

A real partnership, not a sign-up program.

Regular Grip Delivery
Overgrips arrive on your membership schedule. The tier you're offered determines how many and how often, from a quarterly supply through to full monthly competition volume.

Membership Discount
A personal code registered exclusively to you, giving you a reduced rate on your JGG membership subscription. The discount percentage depends on the tier you're selected for.

Player Profile
Considered for a feature on the Just Good Grips website and social channels. Your story, your game and your journey told authentically.
⚡
Early Access
First access to new products, colourways, and upcoming releases before they go public. You see it before anyone else.
📱
Social Features
Consideration for features across our Instagram and Facebook. Showcase your game to a growing audience of players.
📞
Direct Support
No ticketing systems. No waiting. Real support from people who genuinely care about your game and want to see you compete well.
